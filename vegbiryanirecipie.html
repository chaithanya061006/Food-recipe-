Prepareation of Vegetable Biryani

Preparation Time: 20 minutes

Cooking Time: 40 minutes

Total Time: 1 hour

Ingredients:

  • 1.5 cups basmati rice
  • 1 cup mixed vegetables (carrots, peas, beans, potatoes)
  • 1 large onion, thinly sliced
  • 2 tomatoes, chopped
  • 1/2 cup yogurt
  • 2 tbsp cooking oil or ghee
  • 2 tsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1-2 green chilies, chopped (optional)
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric powder
  • 2 tsp biryani masala or garam masala
  • 1/4 tsp saffron soaked in 2 tbsp warm milk (optional)
  • Fresh coriander and mint leaves
  • Salt to taste
  • Whole spices: 2 bay leaves, 4 cloves, 4 green cardamoms, 1 inch cinnamon stick, 1 star anise

Instructions:

  1. Wash and soak the basmati rice for 30 minutes. Drain and set aside.
  2. Heat oil or ghee in a large pan. Add whole spices and sauté for 1 minute.
  3. Add sliced onions and cook until golden brown.
  4. Add ginger-garlic paste and green chilies, sauté for 2 minutes.
  5. Add chopped tomatoes and cook until soft.
  6. Add mixed vegetables, turmeric, biryani masala, and salt. Cook for 5-7 minutes until vegetables are slightly tender.
  7. Lower the heat and add yogurt. Mix well and cook for another 5 minutes.
  8. In a separate pot, boil water with salt and cook rice until 70% done. Drain the rice.
  9. Layer half the rice over the cooked vegetables, sprinkle some fresh coriander, mint, and saffron milk (if using).
  10. Add the remaining rice on top and again sprinkle herbs and saffron.
  11. Cover the pot tightly and cook on low heat ("dum") for 20 minutes.
  12. Turn off heat, let it rest for 5 minutes, then gently fluff the rice.
  13. Serve hot with raita or salad.
