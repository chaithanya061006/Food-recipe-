Preparatiom a Healthy Balanced Meal

Preparation Time: 20 minutes

Cooking Time: 30 minutes

Total Time: ~50 minutes

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup quinoa or brown rice
  • 2 chicken breasts (or tofu for a vegetarian option)
  • 1 cup broccoli florets
  • 1 cup chopped carrots
  • 1 bell pepper, sliced
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt, pepper, and seasoning of your choice (e.g., garlic powder, paprika)
  • Fresh herbs (optional: parsley, cilantro)

Instructions:

  1. Rinse quinoa and cook according to package instructions. Set aside.
  2. Cut chicken into strips. Season with salt, pepper, and paprika.
  3. Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a pan. Cook chicken for 5–7 minutes per side until golden and cooked through. Set aside.
  4. In another pan, heat 1 tbsp olive oil. Sauté vegetables (carrots, broccoli, bell pepper) until tender-crisp, about 6–8 minutes.
  5. Assemble the plate: add quinoa, top with chicken, and add sautéed vegetables.
  6. Garnish with fresh herbs and a squeeze of lemon juice if desired.
  7. Serve warm. Enjoy your balanced, protein-rich meal!
