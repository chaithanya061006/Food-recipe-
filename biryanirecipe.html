Preparation of Biryani

Preparation Time: 30 minutes

Cooking Time: 45 minutes

Total Time: 1 hour 15 minutes

Ingredients:

  • 2 cups basmati rice
  • 500g chicken (or vegetables, or lamb)
  • 1 cup yogurt
  • 2 onions, sliced
  • 2 tomatoes, chopped
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1-inch piece ginger, minced
  • Biryani masala (or garam masala, turmeric, chili powder, cumin)
  • Fresh coriander and mint leaves
  • Saffron soaked in warm milk (optional)
  • Salt and oil or ghee

Calories: 500 kcal

Protein: 25g

Carbohydrates: 45g

Fat: 20g

Fiber: 3g

Cholesterol: 80mg

Sodium: 850mg

Instructions:

  1. Wash and soak the basmati rice for 30 minutes.
  2. In a pan, heat oil and fry the onions until golden brown. Set aside half for garnishing.
  3. Add garlic, ginger, and tomatoes to the pan. Cook until soft.
  4. Add chicken, yogurt, spices, and salt. Cook until the meat is tender and the masala thickens.
  5. In a separate pot, cook the rice until it's 70% done. Drain it.
  6. In a large heavy-bottomed pot, layer rice and chicken masala.
  7. Add fried onions, mint, coriander, and saffron milk on top.
  8. Cover and cook on low flame ("dum") for 15–20 minutes.
  9. Serve hot with raita or salad.
