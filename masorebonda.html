Masore Bonda Preparation

⏱️ Time Taken: 40 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1 cup urad dal (split black gram)
  • 2 green chilies (chopped)
  • 1 inch ginger (grated)
  • 1/2 tsp black pepper
  • Few curry leaves
  • Salt to taste
  • Oil for deep frying

Preparation Steps

  1. Soak urad dal for 4-5 hours and drain well.
  2. Grind to a thick, fluffy batter without adding much water.
  3. Add green chilies, ginger, pepper, curry leaves, and salt.
  4. Heat oil in a deep pan. Wet hands and shape small balls or vadas from the batter.
  5. Fry on medium heat until golden brown and crispy.
  6. Drain on paper towels and serve hot with coconut chutney or sambar.
