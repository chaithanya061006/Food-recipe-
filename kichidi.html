How to Prepare Khichidi

Preparation Time: 10 minutes

Cooking Time: 25 minutes

Total Time: 35 minutes

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup rice
  • 1/2 cup moong dal (yellow split gram)
  • 1 onion, finely chopped
  • 1 tomato, chopped
  • 1 tsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1-2 green chilies, chopped (optional)
  • 1/2 tsp cumin seeds
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric powder
  • Salt to taste
  • 2 tbsp ghee or oil
  • 4 cups water
  • Fresh coriander for garnish

Instructions:

  1. Rinse rice and moong dal together until water runs clear. Soak for 15 minutes (optional).
  2. Heat ghee or oil in a pressure cooker or pot. Add cumin seeds and let them splutter.
  3. Add chopped onions and sauté until translucent.
  4. Add ginger-garlic paste and green chilies, sauté for 1-2 minutes.
  5. Add chopped tomatoes, turmeric powder, and salt. Cook until tomatoes soften.
  6. Add soaked rice and dal. Stir to combine.
  7. Pour in water and mix well.
  8. If using pressure cooker: cook on medium heat for 3-4 whistles. If using pot: cover and cook on low heat until rice and dal are soft (about 25 minutes), stirring occasionally.
  9. Once cooked, let pressure release naturally. Open and stir gently.
  10. Garnish with fresh coriander leaves.
  11. Serve hot with yogurt, pickle, or papad.
