Fried Rice Recipe

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked rice (preferably cold)
  • 1 cup mixed vegetables (carrots, peas, beans)
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 2 cloves garlic (finely chopped)
  • 1 small onion (chopped)
  • 1 tsp ginger (grated)
  • Salt to taste
  • Spring onions for garnish
