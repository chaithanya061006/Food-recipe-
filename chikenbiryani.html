Preparation Grilled Chicken

Preparation Time: 15 minutes

Marination Time: 1 hour (or overnight for best flavor)

Grilling Time: 25–30 minutes

Total Time: ~1 hour 45 minutes

Ingredients:

  • 4 chicken thighs or breasts (bone-in or boneless)
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1 tsp black pepper
  • 1/2 tsp chili flakes (optional)
  • Salt to taste
  • Fresh herbs (like rosemary or thyme, optional)

Instructions:

  1. In a bowl, mix olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, paprika, black pepper, chili flakes, and salt.
  2. Rub the marinade over the chicken thoroughly. Cover and refrigerate for at least 1 hour.
  3. Preheat the grill to medium-high heat.
  4. Place chicken on the grill and cook for about 6–8 minutes per side, depending on thickness.
  5. Check internal temperature (should be 75°C / 165°F).
  6. Let the grilled chicken rest for 5 minutes before serving.
  7. Serve hot with grilled vegetables, salad, or rice.
