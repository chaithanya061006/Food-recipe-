How to Prepare butter chicken

Preparation Time: 15 minutes

Marination Time: 1 hour (or overnight for best flavor)

Grilling Time: 25–30 minutes

Total Time: ~1 hour 45 minutes

Ingredients:

  • 500g boneless chicken (preferably thighs or breast)
  • 1 cup yogurt
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1½ tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 tsp red chili powder (adjust to taste)
  • 1 tsp turmeric powder
  • 1½ tsp garam masala
  • Salt to taste
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 1 large onion, finely chopped
  • 2–3 medium tomatoes, pureed
  • 1 tsp red chili powder (for color and mild heat)
  • 1 tsp coriander powder
  • 1 tsp cumin powder
  • Fresh coriander for garnish

Instructions:

  1. In a bowl, mix olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, paprika, black pepper, chili flakes, and salt.
  2. Add chicken pieces and coat well.
  3. Grill or pan-fry the marinated chicken until it's browned and nearly cooked through (~10-12 mins).
  4. In a pan, heat butter and oil. Add chopped onions and sauté until golden.
  5. Add ginger-garlic paste and cook for 1–2 mins.
  6. Pour in the tomato puree and cook until the oil separates (around 10 minutes).
  7. Add chili powder, coriander, cumin, and salt. Mix well.
  8. Add the grilled chicken to the gravy.
  9. Add ½ cup water (adjust consistency), simmer for 5–10 mins.
  10. Stir in fresh cream and crushed kasuri methi.
  11. Simmer for a few more minutes. Taste and adjust seasoning.
