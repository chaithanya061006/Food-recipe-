Puri Preparation

⏱️ Time Taken: 30 minutes

Ingredients

  • 2 cups whole wheat flour
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1 tbsp oil or ghee (for dough)
  • Water (as needed for kneading)
  • Oil for deep frying

Preparation Steps

  1. Mix flour, salt, and oil. Gradually add water and knead into a firm dough.
  2. Cover and let the dough rest for 15 minutes.
  3. Divide into small balls and roll into 4-5 inch circles.
  4. Heat oil in a deep pan and fry each puri until puffed and golden brown.
  5. Drain on paper towels and serve hot with curry or chutney.
