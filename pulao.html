Preparation of Pulao

Preparation Time: 10 minutes

Cooking Time: 20 minutes

Total Time: 30 minutes

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup basmati rice
  • 1.5 cups water
  • 1 medium onion, sliced
  • 1 cup mixed vegetables (carrots, peas, beans)
  • 2 tbsp oil or ghee
  • 1 tsp cumin seeds
  • 2-3 green cardamoms
  • 1 cinnamon stick
  • 2-3 cloves
  • 1 bay leaf
  • 1 tsp ginger-garlic paste
  • Salt to taste
  • Fresh coriander leaves for garnish

Instructions:

  1. Wash the basmati rice until water runs clear. Soak for 15 minutes, then drain.
  2. Heat oil or ghee in a pan. Add cumin seeds, cardamoms, cinnamon, cloves, and bay leaf; sauté until fragrant.
  3. Add sliced onions and sauté until golden brown.
  4. Add ginger-garlic paste and cook for 1-2 minutes.
  5. Add mixed vegetables and salt, cook for 5 minutes until slightly tender.
  6. Add drained rice and gently stir to combine with veggies and spices.
  7. Add water, bring to a boil, then reduce heat to low. Cover and cook for 15 minutes or until rice is done.
  8. Turn off heat, let it rest for 5 minutes, then fluff with a fork.
  9. Garnish with fresh coriander and serve hot.
