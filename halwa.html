halwa Preparation

⏱️ Time Taken: 10 hours (including soaking and fermentation)

Ingredients

  • 1 cup urad dal (split black gram)
  • 2 cups idli rice
  • 1/2 tsp fenugreek seeds
  • Salt to taste
  • Water as needed

Preparation Steps

  1. Soak urad dal and fenugreek seeds in water for 4 hours.
  2. Soak idli rice separately for 4 hours.
  3. Grind urad dal to a smooth batter. Grind rice to a slightly coarse batter.
  4. Mix both batters, add salt, and let ferment overnight (8 hours).
  5. Pour batter into greased idli molds and steam for 10-12 minutes.
  6. Serve hot with chutney and sambar.
