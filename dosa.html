Dosa Preparation

⏱️ Time Taken: 15 minutes (excluding batter fermentation)

Ingredients

  • 1 cup rice
  • 1/4 cup urad dal (split black gram)
  • 1/2 tsp fenugreek seeds
  • Salt to taste
  • Water as needed
  • Oil or ghee for cooking

Preparation Steps

  1. Wash and soak rice, urad dal, and fenugreek seeds for 6 hours.
  2. Grind them to a smooth batter and let it ferment overnight.
  3. Add salt and mix well. Heat a non-stick pan or tawa.
  4. Pour a ladle of batter and spread it in a circular motion.
  5. Drizzle a few drops of oil or ghee and cook until golden brown.
  6. Fold and serve hot with chutney or sambar.
